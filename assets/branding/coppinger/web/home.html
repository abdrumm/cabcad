About Us

In 1997 Mike Coppinger founded Coppinger Exhibits out of a passion to continually learn new techniques in building and transferring that knowledge into designing and producing experiential events and environments for customers worldwide.

By building an experienced team of professionals, CE was able to quickly demonstrate its unique ability to dissect a marketing strategy to create and implement effective, efficient and out of the box solutions for its clients.

With a focus on the whole trade show program, and not just a singular booth build, Coppinger Exhibits continuously outperforms expectations and continues to experience its own brand growth from 12,000 sq. ft.,20 years ago, to a 76,700 sq.ft. state-of-the art facility today.
